🌍 <B>Research Focus</B>
<BR><BR>
My research brings together <B>machine learning, health informatics, and population science</B> to address challenges in linking and harmonising data from HDSS, clinical, and other longitudinal sources.
<BR><BR>
I’m particularly interested in building solutions that enable <B>cross-site and cross-country integration of health data</B>, improve data quality, and support reproducible epidemiological research within the <B>OHDSI ecosystem</B>.
<BR><BR>
A major goal of this work is to make complex population and clinical data more <B>interoperable, reusable, and research-ready</B>, particularly in <B>low- and middle-income settings</B> where heterogeneous data sources can make large-scale epidemiological research challenging.
